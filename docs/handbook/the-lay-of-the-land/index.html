The Lay of the Land

A visual map of the organizational landscape — the territories, the paths between them, and how to read the terrain.

Before you can navigate, you need a map.

This chapter presents the organization as a landscape — three territories connected by paths. The map on the next page shows the spatial relationships. The pages that follow describe what you will find in each territory and how to move between them.

A field guide does not give you an org chart. It gives you a sense of the ground.
