Ways of the Work

How the organization communicates, decides, and collaborates. This is not a policy document — it is a field guide to getting things done.

Every organization has a way of working. Most of it is unwritten. This chapter makes it explicit.

The pages that follow describe how we talk to each other, how decisions get made, and the rhythm of the working week. These are not rules. They are patterns — observed, tested, and named so you can recognize them.

The goal is not conformity. The goal is clarity. When everyone understands how things work, more energy goes into the work itself and less into figuring out the system.
