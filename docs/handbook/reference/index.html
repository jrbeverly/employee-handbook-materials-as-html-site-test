About This Guide

This field guide was made to help you find your way. It is compact on purpose — a field guide should fit in a pocket, not fill a shelf.

Every section is written to be read in any order. Start with what you need now and return when you need more. The marginal notes are there to help, not to distract.

How It Was Made

This handbook was designed as a proof of concept: to show that an employee handbook can be more than a policy portal or a wiki. It can be an authored, designed, intentional object that introduces a place and its people.

The visual language draws from field guides and instruction booklets — printed things made with care. The typography, palette, layout, and illustration style all serve the metaphor of a guide to unfamiliar territory.

Colophon

Set in Crimson Pro, a Garamond-inspired serif designed by Jacques Le Bailly. Marginal notes in Caveat, by Pablo Impallari. Built with Hugo. Composed as pages and spreads, not as an infinite scrolling column.

The field guide metaphor is a lens, not a straitjacket. Where a diagram works better than a page spread, the diagram wins. Where an annotation clarifies better than a paragraph, the annotation wins. Form follows function — but the form itself carries meaning.
