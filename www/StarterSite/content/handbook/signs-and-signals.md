Every field guide explains its symbols. This chapter is the key — a reference for the marks, notations, and conventions used throughout the handbook.

The pages that follow map each symbol to its meaning, point you to references for deeper exploration, and close with a note on how this guide was made.

A field guide rewards the reader who returns. If a symbol was unfamiliar on first reading, you will recognize it now.
