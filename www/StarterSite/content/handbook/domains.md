## The Organization in Three Domains

The organization is structured around three domains. Each is a lens through which the work of building, delivering, and growing happens.

**Product** is about what we build and why it matters. This domain covers the things we make, the problems we solve, and the people we serve. Every decision about what to work on next traces back to product thinking.

**Craft** is about how we work and what we hold ourselves to. This domain covers engineering standards, design practices, collaboration norms, and the bar for quality. Craft is not about perfection — it is about deliberate, improving practice.

**People** is about growth, support, and connection. This domain covers hiring, onboarding, feedback, career development, and the culture we build together. People work is everyone's work, not just a function.
